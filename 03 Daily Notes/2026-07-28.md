### Hike or Hold? Fed Meeting Will Surprise Either Way
source: [investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/hike-or-hold-fed-meeting-will-surprise-either-way-12028793)
#### Summary
The upcoming Federal Reserve meeting has become one of the most uncertain policy events in recent years. Unlike previous meetings, where policymakers carefully guided market expectations in advance, investors now face genuine uncertainty over whether the Fed will leave interest rates unchanged or deliver a surprise rate hike. Rather than focusing solely on the policy decision, markets are increasingly trying to understand the Fed's new communication strategy under Chair Kevin Warsh.

The bullish interpretation is that greater policy uncertainty reflects a more data-dependent Federal Reserve. By avoiding explicit forward guidance, the Fed preserves flexibility to respond to changing economic conditions while preventing markets from becoming overly reliant on central bank signals. If inflation continues to moderate, policymakers could justify holding rates steady without undermining their credibility.

However, the lack of clear guidance also introduces new risks. Persistent core inflation, rising energy prices, and geopolitical tensions have kept the possibility of another rate hike alive. Investors have become accustomed to predictable Fed communication, and a surprise policy decision could trigger significant volatility across equities, bonds, and currencies.

Ultimately, the debate is no longer just about whether the Fed hikes or holds. The more important question is whether policymakers are deliberately reshaping market expectations by embracing strategic ambiguity. If this communication style becomes the new normal, investors may need to prepare for a more volatile policy environment where uncertainty itself becomes a policy tool.
