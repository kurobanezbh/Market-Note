### Nasdaq Slips as Chip Stocks Drop, Dow Rises; Oil Prices Plunge on Pause in US-Iran Hostilities
source: [investopedia](https://www.investopedia.com/stock-market-today-dow-jones-s-and-p-500-07272026-12027190)
#### Summary
Monday's trading session highlighted a market that is becoming increasingly selective rather than broadly bullish. Although the Dow Jones Industrial Average and the S&P 500 finished modestly higher, the Nasdaq under performed as weakness in semiconductor stocks weighed on technology shares. The divergence suggests that investors are beginning to differentiate between sectors instead of simply chasing market momentum, particularly after two consecutive weeks of declines.

On the positive side, broader market sentiment remains constructive. The easing of geopolitical tensions in the Middle East has pushed oil prices lower, reducing concerns about inflation and supporting expectations that economic growth can remain resilient. Meanwhile, investors continue to anticipate strong earnings from major technology companies, believing that solid corporate fundamentals could help sustain the equity rally despite recent volatility.

However, several risks continue to cloud the outlook. The recent selloff in chip makers reflects growing scrutiny of AI-related valuations following mixed earnings results and rising capital expenditure concerns. In addition, this week's Federal Reserve meeting and a series of earnings releases from major technology companies could significantly influence market expectations for interest rates and corporate profitability.

Ultimately, the market appears to be transitioning from a liquidity-driven rally to one increasingly supported by fundamentals. Whether equities can resume their upward trend will depend less on investor optimism and more on whether earnings and macroeconomic data can justify current valuations.
