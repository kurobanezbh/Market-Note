# Research-AI

## Purpose

Research-AI is a personal research workflow system designed for financial research, market analysis, and knowledge management.

The goal is to combine:

- Financial research
- AI assistance
- Obsidian knowledge management
- Python automation
- Git version control


---

# Workflow

Information Source

↓

↓

↓

Obsidian Knowledge Base

↓

# Folder Structure

## 01 Knowledge

Long-term accumulated knowledge.

Examples:

- Financial concepts
- Investment frameworks
- Industry knowledge
- Macro concepts


## 02 Research

Research projects and investment analysis.

Examples:

- Company research
- Industry research
- Commodity analysis
- Valuation notes


## 03 Daily Notes

Daily market notes.

Examples:

- Investopedia news summary
- Market observations
- Investment thoughts
- Daily learning notes


## 04 Projects

Long-term projects and system development.

Examples:

- Research-AI development
- Stock Screener
- Python projects
- AI automation
- Personal investment research
- Database construction


## 90 Library

Raw materials and references.

Examples:

- PDF reports
- Books
- Articles
- Research documents
- External sources
